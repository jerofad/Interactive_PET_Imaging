An interactive guide to positron emission tomography

From decay
to voxel.

A PET scanner detects pairs of photons. From millions of such events, it infers where a tracer went. Follow that signal through ten stages, each with a working model you can change.

  1. 1TracerA labelled molecule accumulates where the biology takes it.
  2. 2EmissionA positron travels, annihilates, and two photons leave.
  3. 3DetectionCrystals turn each photon into a timed pulse with an energy.
  4. 4CoincidencePairs within a few nanoseconds become lines of response.
  5. 5DataLines are binned into sinograms or kept as list mode.
  6. 6Time of flightArrival-time differences narrow each line to a segment.
  7. 7CorrectionsAttenuation, scatter, randoms and efficiency enter the model.
  8. 8ReconstructionAn iterative fit finds activity that explains the counts.
  9. 9Image qualityResolution and noise limit what an uptake number means.
  10. 10ArtifactsModel mismatches leave recognisable fingerprints.
