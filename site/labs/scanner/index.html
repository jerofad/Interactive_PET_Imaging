Tracer, detector and acquisition settings

Longer FOV accepts more oblique lines; smaller crystals increase detector count. Crystal depth and DOI change the off-axis resolution budget. Material selects cited energy-resolution and light-decay examples. Rendered detector blocks group neighbouring crystals; the exploded view resolves an 8 × 8 array. Detector capture uses each material’s 511 keV attenuation coefficient (LSO value for LYSO; Gonzalez-Montoro et al. 2022, Table 1). Count rates scale with each isotope’s positron fraction. Model assumptions

The 3D histories use approximate 4 mm attenuation integration and μ at 511 keV even after scattering. Random candidates pair unrelated emissions, conditioned on the timing window. The slice panels instead accumulate Poisson increments from a separate 2D model with scan-averaged rates, toy scatter shape and ideal known correction expectations. The analytic 2D-mode rate factors are assumed (0.18 for trues and an additional 0.35 for scatter); FOV uses central-point solid angle and detector depth uses exponential capture. These are illustrative scalings, not scanner specifications; this is not GATE, Geant4 or SimSET. With TOF off, an assumed 600 ps timing jitter still applies to the coincidence test for every material.

Model assumptions

Direct pairs join the same detector ring; oblique pairs connect different rings. Maximum ring difference limits accepted obliquity. Model assumptions

Plain backprojection spreads each line over the image, producing the familiar 1/r blur in the ideal continuous 2D case. Its frames are peak-normalised for display. Decay correction refers the estimate to the phantom’s assigned reference activity; it does not restore lost counts.

Model assumptions

t = 0 is annihilation. The decay-to-annihilation interval is shown as a conceptual prelude: positron slowing and annihilation lifetime depend on material, and positronium can introduce nanosecond delays. No universal isotope-only delay is asserted. Pulses use an assumed 0.5 ns rise, the cited material decay, a 20% CFD fraction and a 5τ energy gate. A 500 ns delayed copy illustrates the principle; it rejects this true pair. Random estimates require other, unrelated singles. These are teaching electronics, not a commercial timing chain.
