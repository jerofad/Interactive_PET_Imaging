Lab / Volume projection

Turn the volume in your hand.

A maximum-intensity projection (MIP) keeps the highest activity encountered along each sampled viewing ray. Rotate around the superior–inferior axis to see how structures overlap.

Projection of the embedded 3D activity volume, not a reconstruction from measured data. A 2 × 2 × 2 maximum pool produces a 64 × 64 × 48 grid at 4 mm spacing; rays are sampled every 2 mm using nearest voxels. Pooling preserves hot maxima but broadens small structures. This approximate MIP is for exploration, not quantification.

At 0°: R left, L right. Superior stays at the top. Other angles combine anatomical directions. Rotation starts only when requested and pauses when the tab is hidden.
